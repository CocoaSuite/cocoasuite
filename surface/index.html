What it does

Paint in 3D

Projected painting on the GPU, straight into the UV textures, with pressure, a stabiliser, mirror and seam padding.

Layers and masks

Paint and fill layers, groups, blend modes and masks, each with its own stack of effects.

CocoaWorlds materials

Drop a .cocoamat into a fill layer and edit every exposed parameter live.

Mesh map baking

Normal, ambient occlusion, curvature, thickness, position and ID, baked on your GPU.

Smart masks

Edges, cavities and thin parts become masks, with edge wear from clouds, cells, grain or scratches.

Export anywhere

PNG maps per texture set with packed ORM, or a .cocoamat that opens in CocoaWorlds.
