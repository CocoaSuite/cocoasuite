CocoaBaker

Mesh maps, baked fast on your GPU.

Bake normal, ambient occlusion and curvature maps from a high-poly model onto a low-poly one, and watch them appear as they bake.

DownloadComing soon

What it bakes

Normal maps

Transfer surface detail from the high-poly model onto the low-poly one.

Ambient occlusion

Clean, low-noise occlusion with a denoising pass.

Curvature

Built from the normal map, with an edge angle setting that keeps low-poly facets from showing as false edges.

Automatic cage

The cage is measured for you, so round high-poly shapes bake cleanly onto low-poly ones.

Bake lights

Place and drag point lights and see their shadows live before you bake.

Live preview

Watch each map appear as it bakes, and choose which meshes cast occlusion.
